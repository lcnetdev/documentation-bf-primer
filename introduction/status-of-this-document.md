# Status of this document

This document is current as of 1 September 2026.

It is anticipated that this Primer will be more a living document than a static one. While the BIBFRAME ontology and vocabulary change infrequently, the flexibility of the ontology and vocabulary means data can be expressed in a variety of ways, especially as needed to support bibliographic description, and even more especially when considering different formats of material.

Given the flexibility of the BIBFRAME model and vocabulary, combined with the infinite web of information, it is impossible to capture every BIBFRAME permutation for every format and scenario. The objective of this document is to provide a solid starting point for implementing the BIBFRAME model and vocabulary, including real-life examples and implementation considerations. It is not comprehensive, but it is expected to be augmented, and otherwise modified, so as to remain reasonably current and, therefore, demonstrative.
