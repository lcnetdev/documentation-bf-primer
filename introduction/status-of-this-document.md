# Status of this document

This document is current as of 2 September 2026.

The objective of this document is to provide a solid starting point for implementing the BIBFRAME model and vocabulary, including real-life examples and implementation considerations. The flexibility of the model and vocabulary means data can be expressed in a variety of ways, especially as needed to support bibliographic description, and even more especially when considering different formats of material. As such, this document is not comprehensive, but will be augmented, and otherwise modified, overtime so that it remains reasonably current and, therefore, demonstrative.
