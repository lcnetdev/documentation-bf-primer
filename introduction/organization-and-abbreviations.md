# Organization and Abbreviations

This document begins with a wide, broad discussion of the BIBFRAME Model in general and proceeds to narrower topics, such as a common BIBFRAME classes and properties followed by narrower discussions about format specific classes and properties. The last section of the document covers particulars about RDF (datatypes, URIs, blank nodes) and other specifics (such as the application of BCP47 language tags). This document is designed to be consumed in this order.

Numerous abbreviations are used throughout. Many will be familiar to the reader (RDF; Resource Description Framework) or obvious (BF; BibFrame) or common to the community (RDA; Resource Description and Access). All should be defined when first used in the document. Some concepts, such as “work” or “item,” have specific meanings depending on context of use. A BF Work is not (necessarily) the same as an RDA Work. When referencing a specific type of entity in the BIBFRAME model (i.e. a Class), the term will be minimally capitalized, but may be preceded by a clarifying prefix (BF Item; RDA Item). If a non-BIBFRAME class or entity is referenced, it will always be preceded by a clarifying abbreviation. For example, when an RDA concept is referenced, the term will always be preceded by the letters RDA.
